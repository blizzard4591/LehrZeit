LehrZeit records your working time as a teacher, quickly and without an account.

Start a timer with one tap on a tile, for example "Correcting › 7b" or simply "Lesson prep". Pause and stop it from the notification or the home-screen widget. If a timer keeps running because you forgot it, LehrZeit asks after a few hours whether you're still working.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Activities such as lesson prep, correcting or meetings, optionally per class</li>
<li>A home screen with General, your favorites and the combinations you use most</li>
<li>An ongoing notification with a live timer, Pause and Stop</li>
<li>A home-screen widget</li>
<li>Worklog: correct start and end times with a tap, add missed entries and notes</li>
<li>Stats per day, week and month, by activity or by class</li>
<li>Export to CSV, ODS (spreadsheet) and PDF (report)</li>
<li>Backup and restore as a file</li>
<li>German and English</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
Your data stays on your phone. LehrZeit has no ads and no tracking, and it needs no account. It only goes online if you enter a timetable link (e.g. from WebUntis): it then downloads that timetable once a day and sends nothing else.
